Sonorama plays looping ambient sounds and mixes them live from pages of touch cards. It is made for reading stories to children and for running tabletop role-playing games: one hand is enough, without looking away from the book or the table.

• A grid of cards: one tap starts or stops a sound, always with a fade.
• A plan mode: prepare several changes, then apply them together with a crossfade.
• A volume per card: long-press, then slide up or down.
• Up to 12 sounds at once, across several pages.
• Your own pages: gather your favourite cards, save a whole mix as a single card.
• Plays with the screen off, with controls on the lock screen and headsets.
• 24 sounds included: nature, city, technology, weather, transport.
• More sounds as ZIP packs imported from the phone; the format is open.

No account, no ads, no tracking. The app requests no network access.
